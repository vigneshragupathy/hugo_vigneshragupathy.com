### <span style="color:#6366f1">**Year 2025 Reflection**</span> 

- Resumed writing blogs after a long gap of nearly 3 years.

- Completed a challenging trek at Gingee Fort with my wife and two kids, climbing both Rajagiri and Krishnagiri hills on the same day.

![Gingee Fort](../../images/2026/trekking.webp)

- My kids (aged 3 and 7) ran their first mini marathon.

![kids Marathon](../../images/2026/kids_marathon.webp)

- Completed my first 10-miler twice, once in Chennai and once in Bangalore. The Bangalore run, done in the rain, was something special.

![10 Mile](../../images/2026/10_miler.webp)

- Attempted my first half marathon; although knee pain at 10K forced me to walk the rest, I completed the race despite missing the 2:30 cutoff.

- Shifted residence from Chennai to Bangalore ([I have already written a blog post on this](../../moving-back-to-bangalore-a-new-chapter-after-4-years-of-wfh)).

- Convinced my wife and switched to a full bald look; my kids accepted it, but playful jokes are now part of daily life.

![Bald](../../images/2026/bald.webp)

- Taught my 8-year-old son how to cycle.

- Enjoyed several family road trips including Blue Flag Kovalam, Mahabalipuram, and Pondicherry.

<span style="color:#ea580c">**The years ahead are simple in my mind: better health, more time with loved ones, and more reasons to smile. Just living, slowly, and going with the flow.**</span>
